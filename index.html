02

Match Systems

New rules for team switching and team kills, squad and rebuy quality-of-life, server seeding rewards, ping limits, and the Lonestar faction experiment.

Team Switching Limits

These apply once a team is more than 10 points ahead, to stop late switches deciding matches:

  • After joining a team you can't switch again for 15 minutes.
  • Switching or joining mid-match keeps your map hidden until you first reach the control zone.
  • You can't use the spawn vehicle until you first reach the control zone.
You can still join your friends' team. These rules just add friction for switch abusers.

Team-Kill Punish / Forgive

  • When a teammate kills you, you choose Forgive or Punish.
  • Punishes stack up against the team killer, leading to kicks and then escalating bans.
  • Forgive removes a potential mark against them.
Respawning and leaving the safe zone without voting counts as forgiving.

Squads & Rebuy

Squad intent

  • Squad leaders pick an intent from a preset list. Everyone sees it in the squad list before joining.
  • Squad invites and other timed prompts now queue.

Rebuy last loadout

  • The old placeholder rebuy UI is gone.
  • Rebuy now fills the vendor with your last loadout, so you can add or remove items before buying.

Server Seeding & Pre-Match

  • Players who fill up empty servers earn rewards and a vendor discount once the match starts.
  • Pre-match activities in each spawn: guns with targets, a football and a hidden radio.

Region Ping Limits

  • Official servers set a maximum ping per region. Starting values are lenient.
  • Staying over the limit for a sustained period gets you kicked. Short spikes and server-side lag don't count.
  • After a high-ping kick you can't rejoin that server until the next map.
  • The server browser gets max-ping tags and filters.
  • Community admins can set their own limits, looser or stricter.

Bakurani Spawns

  • All three team spawns have been moved and rebuilt.
  • Each has purpose-built routes to the zone, for more even matches between teams.

Faction Balance Experiment Season 2 only

Lonestar (blue) had a noticeably lower win rate than the other two factions. Uniform visibility and spawns played a part, but the developers say player sentiment about the blue team was the biggest reason people avoided it. Season 2 tests whether paying Lonestar more changes that.

03

Combat Balance

High-calibre rounds were giving near-sniper damage at assault-rifle fire rates. Base damage drops for those calibres. Snipers get higher hit-location damage so they stay the big single-shot class. The FAL still hits hardest of the assault rifles, but now sits closer to the TCX-SPR and M4.

Calibre Base Damage

These apply to every weapon using the round, including vehicle weapons.

Vehicle guns affected include the Little Bird AH-6M, Z20 Lakota and Humvee miniguns.

Armor & Helmets

Level 4 helmet extras

  • Muffled audio and proximity chat, restricted field of view, fogs up while sprinting.

Weapon Archetypes

DMRs

  • Hit-location damage retuned for the new base values. Headshots and upper-body hits are a little weaker.
  • Meant to sit between assault rifles and snipers, not act as a faster-firing sniper.
  • New recoil pass across all DMRs.

Assault rifles & LMGs

  • Upper, middle and lower torso damage is now more even, so body shots are more predictable.
  • Assault rifle body shots do slightly less damage.
  • LMG body damage now matches assault rifles. LMGs win through sustained fire and magazine size.

Scout Rifle

  • One-shot headshot on targets with no helmet.
  • Two headshots against a Level 1 helmet.

Suppression Rebuilt

  • Shared falloff curves per weapon, so being shot at feels the same whatever is shooting.
  • Near misses from small and medium calibres suppress much harder.
  • Fire rate is factored in, so SMGs and pistols don't suppress too much.
  • Screen shake from suppression is reduced.
  • Level 3 helmets cut suppression by 68%, Level 4 by 80%.

Vehicle turrets vs infantry

  • Heavy and Artillery top machine-gun turrets used helicopter-cannon hit-location values. They now use assault rifle / LMG values.

Parachutes & Medical Bag

  • Parachutes take longer to open fully, and won't open below about 10m.
  • Opening below roughly 50m while falling fast won't slow you in time, so you take fall damage.
  • Trust your pilot, or drive or fly to the zone yourself.

Some weapon inspects are silent. This is a known issue.

04

Building

A full pass on costs, build speed and durability, so structures are an investment rather than spam. The Talon, Mortar and Drill Rig are slower to build and more expensive to keep running.

Build Supply Costs

Drill Rig

Shorter uptime and more fuel needed, so multi-rig builds stop dominating without being banned outright.

Build speed

  • Medium hammer adds less per swing.
  • Critical spots give a smaller bonus.
  • Large hammer is unchanged.

Talon & Mortar

New mortar animation: the shell is dropped into the tube as it fires, which slows the fire rate.

Other Building Changes

  • Barbed wire and sandbags can go on top of Bremmer walls.
  • Barbed wire kills no longer give cash or XP.
  • Foundations fit properly against slopes, stairs and walls.
  • Unfinished buildables collapse as soon as they lose support instead of floating.
  • Tank traps only place on a defined set of surfaces.
  • Hammers can no longer breach doors and gates. The Halligan bar breaches both.
  • CIWS cannon now shreds light cover but struggles against heavy fortifications.
  • New: Wooden Step (small hammer+), Large Shelter (medium hammer+), MG Nest (large hammer).
05

Vehicles

Vehicle Changes

  • Purchased vehicles' inventories are private to the driver and their squad by default. A new setting opens them to everyone once the vehicle is fully unlocked.
  • Free team-spawn vehicles keep open inventories. Use at your own risk.
  • Tracked vehicles feel heavier: less floaty in the air, less bouncy on landing.
  • Humvee handling reworked: less stiff over terrain, slower to reach top speed, climbs better.
  • Dune Buggy brakes are weaker, so it skids and drifts a bit more.
  • Explosives and placeables can't be used from enclosed seats.
  • Vanguard CIWS takes 2.25x longer to kill the spawn vehicle.

Vehicle Fixes

  • Humvee gunner no longer faces the wrong way after entering.
  • Gunner fire desync and inconsistent firing audio fixed.
  • Helicopter cockpit glass works both ways: shooting out through it damages the vehicle like incoming fire.
  • Tanks no longer launch themselves off their own turret collision.
  • Vehicles no longer sink into the ground when ramming at speed.
  • Wheeled vehicles no longer get stuck driving out of a backward roll.
  • Smoke screens and flares now show for late joiners.
  • Exiting places you on the ground instead of pushing you through it.
  • Gamepad: fixed vehicle spinning after closing the tac map, inconsistent ADS on vehicle weapons, and crouch/seat swap in mounted turrets.

  • Look direction recovers faster after closing radial wheels.
  • Faster list scrolling. Shorter holds for 'Purchase Loadout' and 'Apply Changes'.

Fixes

  • Cooking grenades with X works again.
  • Releasing LT now cancels a bandage self-heal.

Every variant now has its own reward. Armed variants give 1.5x the XP of the default, and attack vehicles pay more the more kills they've racked up.

Supply Crate Destroyed

Spotting

  • About 30% lower than Season 1. It was the lowest-risk, highest-paying way to earn.
  • Now scales with the value of each vehicle variant, buildable and crate spotted.
  • Spotted target destroyed by an enemy faction: 50% → 25% of the reward.

Exploit Fixes

  • Spotting rewards on buildables scale with build progress, so spotting unfinished builds can't be farmed.
  • Destroying a buildable pays based on how built it is.
  • Selling from a supply crate has diminishing returns per buyer, down to break-even with no profit or XP.
  • Selling to an enemy player (e.g. after they switch teams) gives no cash or XP.
  • Destroying an empty enemy crate gives half cash and half XP.
  • Crate deploy and destroy rewards scale with the supplies inside.
  • FOB Supplied Assist is split between assisting players instead of paying everyone in full.

Vendor Prices

In-match purchase prices. Armor, high-end weapons and armed vehicles cost a lot more. Backpacks, most attachments and several staple weapons got cheaper, with most muzzles, grips and foregrips down by up to a third.
